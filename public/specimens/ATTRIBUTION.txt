Adaptations in this application: hand-traced boundaries in src/morphology.js;
piecewise registration of separate dorsal/ventral photographs to four shallow curved
wing meshes; view-dependent face culling; image areas outside each wing are excluded
by geometry, not by removing light-colored scale patches. Boundary vertices only
are moved inward off the near-white background, with a 1.5px safety inset; this is
an approximate contour, not an exact pixel-level specimen measurement. The entire image is not
displayed as a flat butterfly billboard. Original scale bars are outside the wing
meshes; this is NOT a physical measurement tool.

The photographic textures, traced outline/registration data and their rendered
adaptations are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0. Retain this attribution and the license
when redistributing or adapting them. No MHNT/photographer endorsement is implied.
This asset notice does not assign a license to unrelated application code.

Methods and limits:
The dorsal outline determines each mesh; the corresponding ventral outline is
registered to it. Left/right pattern differences in the source photographs are
retained. Wing-root areas hidden by the thorax and small overlaps are incomplete.
Venation and markings are photographic surface evidence, not a 3D vein scan.
No artificial radial lines, decorative circles, or copied dorsal texture on the
ventral face are added. The Morpho hue records the lighting of the specimen photo;
angle-dependent optical spectra are not reconstructed.
Body thickness, segment volumes, leg joint positions and shallow wing camber are
approximate reconstructions. Reduced forelegs and four walking legs follow the
family anatomy, not an invented six-equal-leg plan. Specimen wear and taxonomic
variation are described in the interface. This learning atlas is not validated for
research measurement or standalone species identification.

Body refinement, 10 September 2026:
The original MHNT photographs were inspected at full resolution for head/thorax
proportions, dorsal and ventral body markings, scale cover, and abdomen profile.
New body surfaces use hand-authored procedural color/relief and attached short
scale geometry, not additional photographic body textures. Body pattern/profile
adaptations based on the MHNT images retain the above CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution.
